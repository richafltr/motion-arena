# CMU playground BVH episodes

This directory contains Subject 1, trials 1–10 from the Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, using Bruce Hahne's MotionBuilder-friendly BVH conversion.

| Local file | CMU trial | Original CMU description | Arena caption |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `cmu-playground/01_01.bvh` | 1 | playground - forward jumps, turn around | A person makes several forward jumps, then turns around. |
| `cmu-playground/01_02.bvh` | 2 | playground - climb | A person reaches upward and climbs onto playground equipment. |
| `cmu-playground/01_03.bvh` | 3 | playground - climb, hang, swing | A person climbs up, hangs from both arms, and swings their body. |
| `cmu-playground/01_04.bvh` | 4 | playground - climb | A person steadily climbs upward using both arms and legs. |
| `cmu-playground/01_05.bvh` | 5 | playground - climb, go under | A person climbs, crouches down, and moves underneath an obstacle. |
| `cmu-playground/01_06.bvh` | 6 | playground - climb, sit, dangle legs, descend | A person climbs up, sits, dangles both legs, then descends. |
| `cmu-playground/01_07.bvh` | 7 | playground - climb, sit, dangle legs, jump down | A person climbs up, sits with legs dangling, then jumps down. |
| `cmu-playground/01_08.bvh` | 8 | playground - climb, sit, dangle legs, rock back, lower self to ground | A person climbs, sits and dangles their legs, rocks backward, then lowers to the ground. |
| `cmu-playground/01_09.bvh` | 9 | playground - climb, hang, hold self up with arms straight, swing, drop, sit, dangle legs, go under | A person climbs and hangs with straight arms, swings, drops to sit, then moves underneath. |
| `cmu-playground/01_10.bvh` | 10 | playground - climb, swing, lean back, drop | A person climbs up, swings, leans backward, and drops down. |

Source: <https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/search.php?maincat=2&subcat=1>

BVH conversion mirror: <https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap>
